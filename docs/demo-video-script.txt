TRACENET - 2-MINUTE DEMO VIDEO SCRIPT
=====================================

Spoken narration: about 290 words. That is about 2:00 at a calm pace (145 words per minute).
If you run long, cut the lines marked [OPTIONAL].


BEFORE YOU RECORD (10 minutes)
------------------------------
1. Clear the 47 false accident alerts so the sidebar badge doesn't read "49":
   Unified Alert Center -> Filter: Traffic Collisions -> Select Unacknowledged -> Ack.
2. Warm up every scene once. The first search loads the AI model (10-20 s); the second is instant.
   Run these once:
   - "woman in red saree" on the Search page
   - the Hindi query
   - plate GJ19Y5038
   - one Copilot question
   - open Multi-Cam -> Fusion Replay and press Play
3. Stay online: the Copilot uses the cloud model.
4. Browser setup:
   - full screen (F11)
   - 1920x1080
   - zoom 90-100 %
   - light theme
   - DevTools and other tabs closed
5. Record each scene separately with OBS or Xbox Game Bar (Win+Alt+R), then cut them together.
   Speed up any waiting to 2x in the editor.
6. Do NOT show the following in the video:
   - Journey Map on the Surat cameras (the five videos don't share people, so it shows one camera)
   - accident / abandoned-object alerts
   - the Assault page
   - switching plate OCR to PaddleOCR


SCENE-BY-SCENE
--------------

[0:00-0:12] OPENING - CCTV Stream Wall
On screen: Sidebar -> CCTV Stream Wall. The five Surat feeds play with live boxes.
Say:
  "This is TraceNet, forensic search for smart-city CCTV. These are five real camera feeds from
   Surat: the bus depot, the railway station and three markets. Every person and vehicle is
   detected and tracked on a laptop GPU, and over eleven thousand sightings are indexed for search."

[0:12-0:35] NATURAL-LANGUAGE SEARCH + EXPLAINABILITY
On screen:
  1. Search & Investigate. Type: woman in red saree. Press Search.
  2. Expand "Why this matched" on the first result.
  3. Click Seek & Stream. The video jumps to that moment.
Say:
  "Instead of scrolling through hours of footage, an officer simply describes the person.
   'Woman in red saree' returns ranked candidates from every camera in under a second.
   Each result explains why it matched - visual similarity, the generated caption and which
   attributes were verified - so the officer decides, not the AI. One click opens the exact moment
   in the video."

[0:35-0:47] MULTILINGUAL SEARCH
On screen: Clear the box, type: लाल शर्ट वाला आदमी (or Hinglish: laal shirt wala aadmi).
The translation banner appears, then the results.
Say:
  "Officers can also search in Hindi, Gujarati or Hinglish. The query is translated, shown
   transparently, and logged for audit."

[0:47-1:00] VEHICLE NUMBER-PLATE SEARCH
On screen:
  1. Vehicle Plate Search. Exact mode. Type: GJ19Y5038. The vehicle and its plate cutout appear.
  2. Switch to Estimate and type GJ19Y5030 (one wrong digit). It still finds the vehicle.
Say:
  "Number plates are read automatically during ingestion. Search by exact plate, or by a
   half-remembered one - estimate mode tolerates misread characters."

[1:00-1:20] AI COPILOT WITH HUMAN CONFIRMATION
On screen:
  1. Press Ctrl+K. Type: bhai red shirt wala aadmi dhundo. The answer lists matching sightings.
  2. Type: Assign the abandoned object model to CAM_GT_01.
     The Confirm / Cancel card appears. Click CANCEL (not Confirm).
Say:
  "The AI Copilot answers in plain language - even Hinglish - using the same search tools.
   But it can never act on its own: any change, like reassigning a camera's model, waits for the
   officer's confirmation."

[1:20-1:42] MULTI-CAMERA TRACKING - FUSION REPLAY
On screen:
  1. Multi-Cam Intelligence -> Fusion Replay -> Play all.
  2. Click one car's box. The same ID lights up in all three views and on the ground map;
     everything else dims.
Say:
  "For multi-camera tracking we use the public LUMPI dataset: three synchronized cameras at one
   intersection. Our detector tracks every object in each view and projects it onto a shared
   ground map, so one vehicle keeps a single identity across all cameras. Click any object to
   follow it."

[1:42-1:54] EVIDENCE EXPORT [OPTIONAL - rehearse first, the export dialog is new]
On screen: Back on Search, tick two results -> Export -> Evidence Vault shows the bundle as VERIFIED.
Say:
  "Selected results become a sealed evidence bundle - clips, frames, a SHA-256 manifest and a
   chain-of-custody log. Any tampering is detected on verification."

[1:54-2:00] CLOSE
On screen: Back to the CCTV Stream Wall (or the Situation Overview).
Say:
  "TraceNet: describe it, find it, prove it - with a human in the loop and every search audited."
