LIKELY QUESTIONS - SHORT ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------
Q: Does it identify people automatically?
A: No. It ranks candidates with evidence; an officer confirms. No identity is ever declared by
   the system.

Q: How accurate is cross-camera matching?
A: On LUMPI, precision 1.00 and recall 0.98. On real city footage, appearance alone is
   ambiguous - look-alikes score very close - so we only link a camera when the sighting stands
   out AND the route is physically possible in time. We would rather show nothing than a wrong
   hop.

Q: What does it need to run?
A: One GPU workstation, fully offline. We demoed on a laptop RTX 4060. Shared team storage on
   S3 is optional.

Q: Why should a court trust the export?
A: SHA-256 at intake and on every file in the bundle, a manifest plus custody log, and
   verification that fails on a single changed byte.

Q: Privacy?
A: Role-based access, every search logged with the officer's name, face blurring on export, and
   no predictive or "suspicious intent" scoring - by design.

Q: How does it scale to a city?
A: Edge devices do the light live detection; the archive pipeline scales per GPU node; the
   indexes are per-camera and can be sharded. The pilot validates throughput on real feeds first.

Q: Plate accuracy?
A: Each plate is marked read, blurry or not detected - we never guess. Estimate search finds a
   plate even with misread characters.
